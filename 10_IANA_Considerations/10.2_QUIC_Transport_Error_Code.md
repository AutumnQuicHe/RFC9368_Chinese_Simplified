IANA已经注册了下述“QUIC传输错误码（QUIC Transport Error Codes）”，由[https://www.iana.org/assignments/quic](https://www.iana.org/assignments/quic)维护。

<table border="2" frame="void" rules="none">
  <tr>
    <td>值：</td>
    <td><code>0x11</code></td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td>错误码：</td>
    <td><code>VERSION_NEGOTIATION_ERROR</code></td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td>描述：</td>
    <td>错误协商版本</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td>状态：</td>
    <td>永久</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td>规范：</td>
    <td>RFC 9368</td>
  </tr>
</table>
